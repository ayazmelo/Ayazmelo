Be Part of the Ayaz Melo Experience

✦

Ayaz Melo is a celebration of literature, art, music, culture and ideas. Volunteers play an important role in bringing this experience to life.

Whether you are a student, creative individual or someone who wants to contribute to a cultural event, volunteering offers an opportunity to learn, collaborate and make a meaningful contribution.

Your Contribution Matters

Every successful event depends on teamwork, responsibility and dedication. Become part of a team that works together to welcome guests, support activities and create a memorable experience for everyone.

Our Volunteer Committees

✦

Discover the different areas where volunteers contribute to the successful organization of Ayaz Melo 2026.

01
✦

Registration Committee

Helping register guests and maintain organized registration records.

The team assists with registering guests arriving from different cities, collecting required information and maintaining accurate guest records.
02
✧

Reception Committee

Welcoming guests and helping them find their way around the event.

The team receives guests at the entrance, provides guidance and helps visitors reach the appropriate halls and event areas.
03
◇

Hall Management Committee

Supporting seating arrangements and maintaining an organized audience environment.

The team manages the main hall, guides the audience towards seating areas and helps maintain orderly movement during sessions.
04
❖

Food & Hospitality Committee

Helping with refreshments, food arrangements and guest hospitality.

The team supports food distribution, refreshments and hospitality arrangements to ensure guests are properly looked after.
05
✺

Stage Management Committee

Supporting stage preparation, decoration and event operations.

The team assists with stage decoration, preparation, coordination and smooth operations during performances and sessions.
06
✧

Stall Management Committee

Helping manage stalls and supporting stall-related arrangements.

The team supports stall setup, coordination, visitor guidance and the overall management of stall areas.
07
✦

Volunteer Coordination Committee

Supporting volunteer communication, coordination and task management.

The team helps coordinate volunteers, communicate responsibilities and guide team members throughout the event.
08
◇

Mumtaz Mirza Auditorium Committee

Supporting the overall arrangements and management of Mumtaz Mirza Auditorium.

The team assists with the overall auditorium arrangements, coordination, audience facilities and smooth functioning of the venue.
