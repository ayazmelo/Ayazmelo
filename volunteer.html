Volunteering at Ayaz Melo

Learning, Leadership, Recognition & Responsibility

A Platform for Young Minds

Volunteering at Ayaz Melo is more than participating in a cultural festival; it is an opportunity for young people to learn, grow and take meaningful responsibilities. The festival welcomes enthusiastic university and college students, particularly young people between the ages of 18 and 22, who wish to contribute to a literary and cultural gathering. Volunteers become part of a collaborative team and gain first-hand experience by working in different committees. They learn event management, teamwork, communication, leadership, coordination and problem-solving through practical involvement. They also develop the ability to work under pressure, manage responsibilities and interact respectfully with guests, artists, writers and visitors. Ayaz Melo values their dedication and contributions through certificates of appreciation and recognition. Above all, volunteering offers young people an opportunity to build confidence, develop practical skills and become active contributors to society.

Our Volunteer Committees

Seven dedicated committees working together to make Ayaz Melo 2026 a well-organised and memorable event.

Registration Committee

Responsible for welcoming guests through an organised registration and documentation process.

Explore Committee

The Registration Committee plays an important role in maintaining an organised record of guests and visitors attending Ayaz Melo. Its volunteers welcome visitors at registration points and assist them throughout the registration process. They collect and verify the necessary visitor information and maintain accurate registration records. The committee also supports guest documentation and helps manage registration-related enquiries. Volunteers coordinate with the reception and other relevant teams to ensure a smooth process. Their organised work helps the festival maintain a welcoming and professional registration system.

Reception Committee

Welcoming guests with respect, warmth and hospitality from the entrance to the event venue.

Explore Committee

The Reception Committee represents the spirit of hospitality and respect at Ayaz Melo. Its volunteers welcome guests, writers, artists, speakers and visitors as they arrive at the festival. They guide guests from the entrance gate towards the relevant halls and designated areas. Volunteers provide necessary information and assist visitors with courtesy and patience. They ensure that every guest feels respected, comfortable and welcomed. The committee coordinates with registration and hall management volunteers to support a smooth arrival experience. Its role reflects the cultural values of hospitality, kindness and mutual respect.

Hall Management Committee

Maintaining discipline, seating arrangements and a comfortable environment inside event halls.

Explore Committee

The Hall Management Committee is responsible for maintaining decorum and proper arrangements inside the festival halls. Its volunteers guide guests and visitors towards their designated seating areas. They help maintain organised seating arrangements for women and men according to the event's arrangements. Volunteers communicate politely and respectfully with visitors while assisting them in finding their seats. They help manage movement inside the hall and prevent unnecessary disruption during sessions. The committee works closely with the reception and stage teams to maintain a peaceful environment. Its efforts ensure that audiences can comfortably enjoy every session.

Stage Committee

Supporting stage arrangements, decoration and the smooth conduct of festival sessions.

Explore Committee

The Stage Committee looks after the arrangements and management of the festival stage. Its volunteers assist in preparing and decorating the stage according to the requirements of different sessions. They help maintain the stage environment and coordinate necessary arrangements before each programme begins. Volunteers assist speakers, performers and session participants with stage coordination. They remain attentive to programme requirements and help ensure smooth transitions between sessions. The committee works in coordination with the organising team to address stage-related needs. Its contribution helps maintain an organised, professional and engaging stage experience throughout Ayaz Melo.

Food Committee

Managing food arrangements and ensuring organised distribution among guests and participants.

Explore Committee

The Food Committee is responsible for coordinating food arrangements during Ayaz Melo. Its volunteers assist in organising the distribution of food among invited guests, speakers, artists, volunteers and other designated participants. They help maintain an orderly distribution process and ensure that food reaches the intended recipients. Volunteers coordinate with the organising team regarding timing, arrangements and serving requirements. They also help maintain cleanliness and proper management around food distribution areas. The committee works with care, cooperation and responsibility to support the comfort and hospitality of everyone involved in the festival.

Stall Committee

Coordinating and supporting the various book, literary and cultural stalls at the festival.

Explore Committee

The Stall Committee looks after the management and coordination of stalls established during Ayaz Melo at Khanabadosh. These may include book stalls, publishing stalls, cultural displays and other participating stalls. Volunteers assist stall representatives with basic arrangements and provide guidance regarding stall locations. They help maintain organised movement around the stall areas and respond to visitors' enquiries. The committee coordinates with stall participants and the organising team to address operational requirements. Its work creates a welcoming space where visitors can explore literature, culture, publications and creative activities.

Mumtaz Mirza Auditorium Committee

Managing volunteer responsibilities and supporting the overall arrangements at Mumtaz Mirza Auditorium.

Explore Committee

The Mumtaz Mirza Auditorium Committee is responsible for coordinating volunteer activities and supporting the overall management of the auditorium during Ayaz Melo. Its volunteers assist with guest reception, seating arrangements and visitor guidance within the auditorium. They help maintain discipline and organised movement throughout the venue. The committee coordinates with registration, reception, hall management, stage and other relevant teams according to programme requirements. Volunteers remain attentive to the needs of guests, speakers and audiences. They work collectively to address venue-related arrangements and ensure a smooth experience. Their teamwork helps maintain an organised and welcoming auditorium environment.
