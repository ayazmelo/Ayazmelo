MORE THAN VOLUNTEERING

Be Part of Something Meaningful.

Ayaz Melo brings together literature, culture, art, ideas and people. Volunteers are an important part of making this experience welcoming, organised and memorable.

Through volunteering, students and young people can discover their abilities, take responsibility, gain practical experience and contribute to a shared cultural experience.

Your Contribution Matters

Every role is an opportunity to learn, work with others and make a meaningful contribution. Bring your enthusiasm and become part of the Ayaz Melo volunteer community.

FIND YOUR ROLE

Explore Our Volunteer Committees

Discover the different areas of responsibility and explore how each team contributes to the successful organisation of Ayaz Melo.

01 / COMMITTEE

Registration Committee

Guest registration, information collection and registration records.

EXPLORE COMMITTEE +
Welcomes the registration process for guests arriving from different cities and areas, records their details and helps maintain an organised guest registration system.
02 / COMMITTEE

Reception Committee

Guest welcome, reception and guidance from the entrance to the hall.

EXPLORE COMMITTEE +
Receives guests at the gate, welcomes them and guides them from the entrance to the relevant hall or event area.
03 / COMMITTEE

Hall Management Committee

Audience seating, hall organisation and internal arrangements.

EXPLORE COMMITTEE +
Looks after the main event hall, guides audience members to suitable seats, maintains seating arrangements and supports orderly movement inside the hall.
04 / COMMITTEE

Food & Hospitality Committee

Food, refreshments and hospitality arrangements.

EXPLORE COMMITTEE +
Supports food and refreshment arrangements, coordinates hospitality services and helps ensure guests and authorised participants are properly served.
05 / COMMITTEE

Stage Management Committee

Stage decoration, preparation and stage operations.

EXPLORE COMMITTEE +
Looks after stage preparation and decoration, supports stage setup and coordinates arrangements required for programme activities.
06 / COMMITTEE

Stall Management Committee

Stall arrangements, coordination and day-to-day care.

EXPLORE COMMITTEE +
Looks after event stalls, supports stall arrangements, coordinates with stall representatives and helps maintain organised stall areas.
07 / COMMITTEE

Volunteer Coordination Committee

Volunteer guidance, duty allocation and team coordination.

EXPLORE COMMITTEE +
Coordinates volunteer teams, communicates assigned duties, provides guidance and supports cooperation between different teams.
08 / COMMITTEE

Mumtaz Mirza Auditorium Committee

Overall arrangements and operational care of the auditorium.

EXPLORE COMMITTEE +
Looks after the overall Mumtaz Mirza Auditorium space and its arrangements, monitors auditorium needs and supports smooth operations.
