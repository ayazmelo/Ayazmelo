BEYOND VOLUNTEERING

More Than an Event. An Experience That Helps You Grow.

Ayaz Melo is a celebration of literature, culture, art, ideas and community participation. Volunteers play an important role in making this experience meaningful and successful.

Becoming a volunteer gives you an opportunity to participate in a real event environment, work with a team, take responsibility and discover your abilities.

Every Contribution Matters.

Whether you are a student, a learner or someone who wants to contribute to society, volunteering gives you a chance to learn through experience and become part of something meaningful.
