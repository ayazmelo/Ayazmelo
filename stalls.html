A Meeting Place for Books, Culture & Creativity

The Book & Cultural Stalls at Ayaz Melo provide a vibrant platform for publishers, booksellers, literary organisations, cultural institutions, artists, artisans, craftspeople and creative entrepreneurs from across Sindh.

Every year, Ayaz Melo brings together people from diverse cities, towns and communities. Among them are individuals and organisations working to promote literature, education, traditional craftsmanship, indigenous knowledge, visual arts and Sindhi cultural heritage.

This platform celebrates their contribution by bringing their work closer to readers, visitors, students, researchers and cultural enthusiasts.

It is more than a marketplace. It is a meeting place for knowledge, creativity, cultural exchange and community engagement.

Sindh's Literary Identity

A Tradition of Books, Knowledge & Ideas

Sindh has a rich and diverse literary tradition. Its intellectual heritage has been shaped by poets, writers, scholars, publishers, researchers and literary institutions who have contributed to the preservation and development of Sindhi language and literature.

Publishing houses and booksellers play an important role in connecting authors with readers and making literary works accessible to society. From poetry and fiction to history, philosophy, religion, research and translated literature, books remain an essential part of Sindh's cultural identity.

The Book & Cultural Stalls at Ayaz Melo celebrate this publishing community by bringing together a wide range of publishers, booksellers and literary institutions under one roof.

Sartyoon Sang Crafts Enterprise

Celebrating the skills, creativity and entrepreneurial contributions of rural women artisans.

Sartyoon Sang

Crafts • Creativity • Heritage

Empowering Rural Women Through Craft

Sartyoon Sang Crafts Enterprise, associated with the Sindh Rural Support Organization (SRSO), provides a platform for traditional craftsmanship and the creative work of rural women artisans.

Its products include handmade embroidery, appliqué, patchwork, traditional textiles, decorative products and home décor items that reflect the artistic skills and cultural heritage of Sindh.

By presenting these crafts at cultural events, the enterprise connects rural women producers with wider audiences and creates opportunities to recognise their talent and hard work.

At Ayaz Melo, such initiatives represent the important relationship between culture, creativity, community participation and economic opportunity.
