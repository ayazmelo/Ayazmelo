The historical journey of Ayaz Melo from 2015 to 2025. A living record of literature, ideas, poetry, music, books and the cultural heritage of Sindh.

A Decade of Literature & Culture

Ayaz Melo is a literary and cultural gathering inspired by the life, poetry and intellectual legacy of Shaikh Ayaz. Since its beginning, it has brought together writers, poets, scholars, artists, musicians, students and cultural communities on one platform.


Traditionally, Ayaz Melo is associated with the last weekend of December. Across its editions, the festival has featured literary discussions, poetry sessions, book launches, music, singing, theatre, art exhibitions and cultural performances.


This archive preserves the journey of Ayaz Melo from 2015 to 2025 through historical records, programme details, guests, photographs, posters and media coverage.
