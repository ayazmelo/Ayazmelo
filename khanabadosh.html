What is Khanabadosh?

A Literary and Cultural Space in Hyderabad

A Place for Ideas, Dialogue and Creativity

Khanabadosh Writers' Cafe is an independent literary and cultural space established in Hyderabad, Sindh, in 2015. Located within the premises of the Sindh Museum, it was envisioned as a welcoming meeting place for writers, poets, intellectuals, students, artists and people from different walks of life. Khanabadosh provides an open environment where people can meet, exchange ideas, discuss literature, share creative thoughts and participate in cultural activities. It seeks to revive the tradition of intellectual gatherings and meaningful conversations in Hyderabad. More than a cafe, Khanabadosh represents a shared space built around freedom of expression, inclusivity, creativity, dialogue and cultural connection.

History & Origins

The Beginning of a Literary Journey in 2015

The Beginning

Khanabadosh Writers' Cafe came into existence in 2015 with the vision of creating a shared public space for literature, intellectual discussion and cultural engagement in Hyderabad. The idea was conceived by Professor Amar Sindhu, with collaborative efforts associated with Haseen Shah and Professor Dr. Arfana Mallah. The cafe was established within the premises of the Sindh Museum, providing a space where creative minds and members of the community could come together without barriers of background or identity.

Why the Name Khanabadosh?

The word Khanabadosh refers to a nomadic spirit — one that travels, explores, discovers and remains open to new experiences. The name reflects a spirit of intellectual freedom, curiosity and creative exploration. Like travelling minds that cross boundaries and discover new horizons, Khanabadosh welcomes diverse thoughts, voices and perspectives. It represents a journey of ideas that does not remain confined to one place, one tradition or one way of thinking.

A Space for Everyone

Khanabadosh was envisioned as an inclusive public gathering place where women, young people, students, writers, artists and intellectuals could participate in meaningful conversations and cultural activities. It helped create an environment in which literature, philosophy, art, music and social questions could be discussed openly. Its identity grew around the idea of an open literary gathering place rather than an exclusive institution.

Literary & Intellectual History

Conversations, creativity and cultural expression

A Meeting Ground for Creative Minds

Since its establishment, Khanabadosh has served as a gathering place for literary and intellectual activities in Hyderabad. It has hosted book launches, poetry readings, mushairas, literary discussions, seminars, musical programmes and cultural gatherings. Writers, poets, researchers, students, journalists and artists have come together to exchange ideas and share their work. The cafe has also provided opportunities for emerging voices and young people to engage with literature, philosophy and the arts. Its environment encourages dialogue across generations, languages and creative traditions. Through these activities, Khanabadosh has contributed to the continuing cultural and intellectual life of Hyderabad and Sindh.

Khanabadosh & Ayaz Melo

Khanabadosh Writers' Cafe has been closely associated with Ayaz Melo since its first edition in 2015. Dedicated to the legacy, poetry and intellectual contribution of the legendary Sindhi poet Shaikh Ayaz, the festival brings together writers, poets, scholars, artists, students and audiences from different backgrounds. Through literary sessions, poetry, discussions, music and cultural performances, Ayaz Melo reflects the very spirit of Khanabadosh: freedom of expression, creative exploration and the celebration of literature and culture.
